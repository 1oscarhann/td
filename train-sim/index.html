    How it works

    1. You start at the platform with the doors open. At departure time press D to close them, wait for the right away (two bells), and go when the signal clears.
    2. Hold Space once moving. Press Q within 2.7 s of every AWS horn.
    3. Never pass a red. If you're stuck at one, call the signaller with C.
    4. Sound the horn at "SW" whistle boards before footpath crossings.
    5. Stop at the 4-car marker and press D to open the doors.

    Sound starts when you click. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
