Coastway Driver

You're in the cab of a Class 377 Electrostar at Test Platform A. A train has just left ahead of you, so the first signal is red. Drive to Test Platform B, stop at the 4-car marker and open the doors.

TabShow / hide the driving aid
P / EscPause and settings

How to drive it

  1. You start in brake step 3. Press W to release through B2, B1, OFF and into power.
  2. Hold Space once you take power, or the brakes go on.
  3. A horn means a caution signal ahead: press Q within 2.7 seconds.
  4. Never pass a red signal. TPWS will stop you, hard.
  5. A 75 mph train needs over 600 m to stop in full service. Plan ahead.
