# Where realism and fun clash

Your calls. Each has a default; most are already a setting (press P in game).

| # | Clash | Realistic | Fun / accessible | Default now |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Dead man's handle** | Hold Space the whole time you're moving. Tiring on a keyboard. | "Vigilance": tap Space when it buzzes every 60 s (what most PC sims do). | Hold (setting) |
| 2 | **Route knowledge** | Real drivers memorise every signal, gradient and limit. No on-screen help. | Driving aid: next signal and distance, gradient, braking advice, distance to the stop. | On (Tab / setting) |
| 3 | **Seeing signals** | A lamp 1 km away is a few pixels: readable to an eye, not on a monitor. | Lit lamps never shrink below ~7 px. | On (setting) |
| 4 | **Field of view** | Wide is how a cab feels, but everything far away looks tiny. | Narrower = bigger signals, less peripheral view. | 50° vertical (setting) |
| 5 | **After a penalty brake** | Stop, phone the signaller, wait for authority, then reset. | Stop, power off, press Q. | Simple. A signaller dialogue could come in M3. |
| 6 | **SPAD consequences** | Taken off driving duties, investigation. | Big score penalty, keep going. | Penalty. Option to end the run? Your call. |
| 7 | **Doors away from a platform** | Physically possible, a serious incident. | Doors simply refuse. | Refuse. Could become "allowed, big penalty". |
| 8 | **Acceleration** | 1,200 kW across 184 t: 0–60 mph ≈ 70 s. Might feel sluggish. | An "arcade" multiplier. | Realistic. |
| 9 | **Waiting at reds** | Real time. Can be minutes. | ×2 / ×4 time warp while stationary. | Not built. Want it? |
| 10 | **Braking distances** | 600 m+ from 75 mph. Miss your brake point and you overrun. | Generous stop-marker tolerance. | Realistic distances. The run counts anywhere the whole train is on the platform; you lose 20 points per metre off the marker after the first. |
| 11 | **Low adhesion (later)** | Autumn in the Arun valley can add hundreds of metres to a stop. | Milder slip. | Decide at that milestone. |
| 12 | **Level crossing failures (later)** | Rare. | Frequent enough to matter. | Decide at M5 (a "frequency" setting is easy). |
| 13 | **Route length** | ~25 min Chichester → Amberley with stops. | Start-from-any-station option. | Decide at M2. |
