# Where realism and fun clash

Your calls. Each has a default; most are a setting (press P in game).

| # | Clash | Realistic | Fun / accessible | What it does now |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Dead man's handle** | Hold Space the whole time you're moving. Tiring on a keyboard. | "Vigilance": tap Space when it buzzes every 60 s (what most PC sims do). | Hold (setting: hold / vigilance / off) |
| 2 | **Route knowledge** | Real drivers memorise every signal, gradient and limit. No on-screen help. | Driving aid: next two signals, gradient, speed changes, distance to the stop. | On (Tab / setting) |
| 3 | **Seeing signals** | A lamp 1 km away is a few pixels: readable to an eye, not on a monitor. | Lit lamps never shrink below ~7 px. | On (setting) |
| 4 | **Field of view** | Wide is how a cab feels, but everything far away looks tiny. | Narrower = bigger signals, less peripheral view. | 50° vertical (setting, 40–80°) |
| 5 | **After a penalty brake** | Stop, phone the signaller, wait for authority, then reset. | Stop, power off, press Q. | Simple for AWS/DSD/overspeed. After a SPAD you must call the signaller (C) first. |
| 6 | **SPAD consequences** | Taken off driving duties, investigation. | Big score penalty, keep going. | Penalty and an automatic D grade; setting to end the run instead. |
| 7 | **Doors away from a platform** | Physically possible, a serious incident. | Doors simply refuse. | Refuse. |
| 8 | **Acceleration** | 1,200 kW across 184 t: 0–60 mph ≈ 70 s. Might feel sluggish. | An "arcade" multiplier. | Realistic. No multiplier: time warp covers the boredom. |
| 9 | **Waiting at reds and long runs** | Real time. Can be minutes; the full line is 25 minutes. | Time warp. | ×1 / ×2 / ×4 with T, any time. Physics still steps at 60 Hz, so it's the same drive, faster. |
| 10 | **Braking distances** | 600 m+ from 75 mph. Miss your brake point and you overrun. | Generous stop-marker tolerance. | Realistic distances. A stop counts anywhere the whole train is on the platform; you lose 10 points per metre off the marker after the first (up to 150). |
| 11 | **Low adhesion** | Autumn in the Arun valley can add hundreds of metres to a stop. | Milder slip. | Realistic-ish: μ drops to 0.035 on wet leaves under trees in autumn. Wheel-slide protection and sanders help. Pick another season if it's not fun. |
| 12 | **Failures and trespass** | Rare: years between incidents for one driver. | Frequent enough to matter. | "Rare" (about one every 30 min of driving). Setting: off / rare / normal / frequent. |
| 13 | **Signals** | Real positions and IDs. | Any plausible layout. | Generated layout (the real one isn't published openly). See ASSUMPTIONS G4. |
| 14 | **Timetable** | Real Southern times. | Times that fit the generated signalling. | Generated by driving each service headless, +5 %. A careful driver keeps time; a timid one doesn't. |
| 15 | **AHB crossings** | No signal protects them: you just trust they work. | | Realistic. If one fails, the signaller radios you and imposes 10 mph over it: a failed full-barrier crossing keeps its signal red instead. |
| 16 | **Passing a failed signal** | Signaller dictates a formal message, you repeat it back. | Press C, get authority. | Press C, wait for the answer (about 5 s), use the TPWS override (O). |
